Discover Why Customers Love Our Jewellery Collection

Everything you need to know about our sovereign gold purity, diamond grading, and bespoke commissions.

We exclusively work with pure 22K (916) and 18K (750) BIS hallmarked gold, Platinum 950, and 100% natural, untreated earth-mined gemstones. Every diamond is conflict-free, graded for superior cut, polish, and symmetry, and authenticated by GIA, IGI, or SGL dossiers.

Yes, absolutely. Every piece carries an official government BIS hallmark laser-engraved onto the precious metal. Solitaires above 0.30 ct come accompanied by individual GIA/IGI grading certificates with matching laser-inscribed girdle serial numbers.

Our private atelier specializes in bespoke commissions. You can collaborate directly with our Master Goldsmiths to design one-of-a-kind engagement rings, family crest signets, or bridal Polki chokers. Contact our private salon to begin your custom design sketch.

We provide a 14-day hassle-free return and exchange window. Furthermore, all Mahira jewelry is backed by our permanent 100% gold value exchange guarantee and complimentary lifetime cleaning, polishing, and prong inspection services.
